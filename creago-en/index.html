🎯 The idea in 20 seconds

Claude doesn't "generate" video like AI video apps. It writes code, and the code draws every frame. That's why the video stays clean: nothing melts or jitters, and you can change a single line.

The catch: your first try usually looks like shapes sliding around, because a style is hundreds of decisions (colors, camera, timing, sound). That's where Creago comes in: 50+ ready-made styles studied from viral videos.

Browse the styles

Open the style library. You can watch every style as a video before you decide:

🎬 Open the style library

Styles like wordless drawn stories, maps with a flying camera, ink-and-gold history, and more.

2

Connect Creago to Claude

  1. Creago works with Claude Code (also Codex, Cursor and Gemini CLI).
  2. Buy Creago on their website (one-time payment, no subscription).
  3. In Claude Code, give it this link. It connects by itself; sign in once:
creagoai.com/mcp
3

Type your idea in one line

Pick a style number from the library (for example C001) and ask like this:

Make me a 60-second video about [your idea here], in style C001. Show me the script and the first few seconds before you continue.

Creago shows you the script, the look and the first seconds, and only continues after you approve. Then it animates the scenes, adds voice and music, and saves an MP4.
